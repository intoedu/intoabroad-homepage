KEY POINT

What makes the INTO ABROAD US Schooling Camp special

A 100% Texas private school experience

At a prestigious private Christian school in Texas, students take 7 hours of regular classes a day from outstanding teachers alongside American students. In this program designed for international students, the 24-hour English Policy that continues after school and an English-only community life immerse students in an environment of absorption rather than memorization, so they naturally pick up easy, natural English for all kinds of situations.

Convergence education from art & tech experts

Connecting fields such as drawing, design, AR and interaction, students discover problems around them from a fresh perspective and turn their ideas into real works and a portfolio. Through individual projects and team collaboration they develop creativity, digital skills, communication and problem-solving together, laying the groundwork to grow into future-ready, multidisciplinary talent.

Career mentoring with working professionals & professors

Through counseling and mentoring with working professionals and professors in art and design, students hear firsthand about real majors and careers. Based on their own interests and strengths, they receive concrete advice on further study, choosing a major and how to prepare their portfolio.

Local excursions and building a global network

Visits to RICE University, UT-Austin and TEXAS A&M, the Ivy League of the South, and meetings with real college students help students shape their future career and study plans. Tours of top-10 US cities and Fortune 500 companies let them experience world-class technology trends, and visits to the NASA space center and the State Capitol build a sense of global leadership. There are also field trips 1–2 times a week, including outlet and supermarket visits.

Plus a tour of LA, America's top destination

In LA, home to all of America's top-10 tourist attractions: Hollywood, Griffith Observatory, campus visits to UCLA and USC, and Disneyland. A tour where students see and feel their dreams at a glance (art, culture, media, business, an American network and more), it is a powerful, motivating time that widens their view of the world and wakes up a dormant mind.

Life in the US that is safe, mentally and physically

The camp is located in College Station, a safe area near Houston, Texas, and all travel is by our own direct pickup system, so student safety is managed thoroughly. An education program that expanded to the US, carrying the educational know-how of a Korean alternative school that has produced graduates of leading universities such as Korea University, Sungkyunkwan University and Handong Global University, it fosters character growth. No media! Young mentors from US universities manage the dormitory, and through 1:1 mentoring and small-group meetings students learn to open up and build relationships with people of different ages.

Small groups, close supervision. This is a schooling school run exclusively with IMUSA every year. Students depart as a group (with a chaperone), and on arrival at the TX airport they are met by local Korean guardians and staff.

THE SCHOOL

Cornerstone Christian Academy

Cornerstone Christian Academy is a local private school in Texas, USA, founded in 2001. It offers an elementary, middle and high school curriculum for kindergarten and grades 1 through 12, and, rooted in biblical values with Christ as its head, it teaches sound identity and values. Its professional teachers have extensive experience guiding students and provide balanced care across academics, faith and daily life. Students are placed in the grade-level class that matches their age and take classes there.

CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY PORTFOLIO PROGRAM

Creative Convergence Project Curriculum

Creative Technology Portfolio Program · Artistic expression → Digital expansion → Interaction.
A sample plan fitted to the existing Session 1 camp schedule (Jan 2 – Jan 29, 2027).

01 · Jan 7 – 8

Observation and reinterpretation Observe & Reframe

Students observe everyday life, objects and behavior with fresh eyes and express them through drawing, painting and collage.

02 · Jan 11 – 12

From personal experience to social questions Personal → Social

Students expand personal experiences into social questions and work them out through drawing, typography and graphic art.

03 · Jan 13 – 15

Visualizing the invisible Make the Invisible Visible

Students visualize social phenomena and data with color, form and images.

04 · Jan 18 – 19

Art expanded with AR AR: Hidden Reality

Students express the information and stories held in a place through posters, illustrations and graphics, then expand them with AR.

05 · Jan 20 – 22

Interaction that changes behavior Behavior Intervention

Students design interactions that encourage behavior change using participation, choice and feedback.

06 · Jan 25 – 27

Gamified team hackathon Gamification Hackathon

Teams tackle education, environment, culture and campus problems through game design, then build and test an MVP.

07 · Jan 28

Final portfolio & presentation Final Portfolio

Students organize their project results and process and share them through a demo video and an English presentation, tied in with the camp graduation ceremony.

TRAINING PROGRAM

Training in both study and life

English classes & activities

Everything in English

All activities are conducted in English with native-speaking Americans, and through TOEFL and conversation classes students learn and experience the language and American culture. With TOEFL / vocabulary / conversation / essay editing / English presentation classes, they lose their fear of English and take a step up.

Learning mechanism

A learning system from a study-abroad specialist

An education system built on three pillars, mentoring, reading and self-directed learning, that helps students grow into lifelong independent learners.

Life training

24/7 3C community training

Dormitory Ministry! All of daily life is training. Roll call is a time to recall the community spirit.

Character education

Character education (life management, learning self-discipline)

During the camp, students learn hygiene, space and time management, and within the community they practice managing their language, behavior, habits and relationships to learn the manners people should show one another.
