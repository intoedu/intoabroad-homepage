Q&A

FAQ

Q1Does a parent have to come along?
AYes, this is a Parent-Accompanied camp. While students attend private school classes and ESL, parents take part in the Parent Academy. The parent fee is KRW 2.5 million per person.
Q2Which should we choose, Session 1 or Session 2?
ABoth sessions have the same program. Session 1 departs on Aug 25 and Session 2 on Sep 22, and each lasts 4 weeks. Extensions are available for both sessions.
Q3Do we need to arrange airfare separately?
AYes, airfare is not included in the program fee, so you will need to arrange it separately. Lodging · meals · local transportation · program · insurance are all included; you only need to bring personal spending money.
Q4Can we arrange our own housing or a car?
AWe offer these as options. The premium housing (Airbnb) upgrade costs at least KRW 2 million extra and varies by application date. Car rental costs an extra KRW 1.5 million – 2 million.
Q5Is capacity really just 10?
AYes, we run it as a small group limited to 10. Once full, enrollment closes regardless of the departure date. We'll let you know about remaining spots by phone.
