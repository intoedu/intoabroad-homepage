Texas, Land of Opportunity

07 · SCHOLARSHIPS & RESULTS

A 75% Tuition Scholarship
Open to International Students

Corps of Cadets

Founded together with A&M in 1876, it is the oldest military training organization in the US (about 2,000 cadets) and the only civilian-university corps in the country that runs ROTC for all 4 branches of the US military. International students can apply on the same terms. Those accepted receive up to 75% off tuition · live in the Quad, the Corps' own residence community · and take ROTC leadership · fitness · time-management training alongside their regular studies. Graduates can go straight into private-sector jobs · no military service obligation.
