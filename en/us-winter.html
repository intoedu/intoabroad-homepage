KEY POINT

What makes INTO ABROAD's US Schooling Camp special

The Full Texas Private School Experience

At a prestigious private Christian school in Texas, students take 7 hours of regular classes a day from outstanding teachers alongside American students. Through regular classes with local students, small classes, a safe school environment and a program tailored to international students, plus a 24-hour English Policy that continues after school and an English-only community, students pick up natural English in an environment built on absorption rather than memorization.

Tours of 4 Major Texas Cities · Global Network

Campus visits to the "Ivy League of the South" — RICE University, UT-Austin and TEXAS A&M — and meetings with real college students help turn future career and study plans into concrete goals. Tours of top-10 US cities and Fortune 500 companies showcase world-class technology trends, and visits to the NASA Space Center and the State Capitol build a sense of global leadership. There are also 1–2 field trips every week, including outlets and grocery stores.

Plus a Tour of LA, America's Top Destination

Explore the culture and top universities of the US West Coast: Universal Studios (a theme park of dreams and fantasy), Hollywood (experience the city of movies), UCLA (a campus tour of a top US university), USC (a cradle of global talent) and even Griffith Observatory. A powerfully motivating time to see your dreams in art, culture, media and business all at once.

Parent Academy for Accompanying Parents

Expert lectures covering everything from study-abroad preparation to career planning. With the latest US study-abroad trends, career planning strategies for your child, US student visa (F-1) guidance and preparation, and a one-on-one consultation Q&A, parents see and prepare on site. For KRW 2.5 million per parent (4 weeks), parents live together at the INTO ABROAD US center.

Mentally and Physically Safe Life in the US

The camp is based in College Station, a safe area near Houston, Texas, and students travel everywhere by our own pickup system, so student safety is managed thoroughly. The program builds character by bringing to the US the educational know-how of a Korean alternative school whose graduates have gone on to leading universities such as Korea University, Sungkyunkwan University and Handong Global University. No media! Young mentors from US universities manage the dormitory, and through 1:1 mentoring and small-group meetings students learn to open up and build relationships with people of all ages.

Better English & a Global Mindset

All activities with American native speakers are in English, and TOEFL / vocabulary / conversation / essay feedback / English presentation classes help students lose their fear of English and take a step up. We share career center counseling results and level test results so you can see the change over 4 weeks for yourself.

Small groups, close supervision. This is a schooling partner school we run exclusively every year. Students depart together as a group (with a chaperone), and local Korean guardians and staff meet them on arrival at the Texas airport.

THE SCHOOL

Cornerstone Christian Academy

Cornerstone Christian Academy is a local private school in Texas, founded in 2001. It offers an elementary, middle and high school curriculum for kindergarten and grades 1 through 12, and, rooted in biblical values with Christ as its head, it teaches a sound identity and values. Its professional teachers have extensive experience guiding students and provide balanced care across academics, faith and daily life. Students are placed in the grade-level class that matches their age and attend classes there.

DESTINATIONS

LA · Texas Destinations

Walk through the culture and top universities of the US West Coast, and Texas, a fast-rising financial hub and part of the Bible Belt.

LA

Universal Studios · Hollywood · UCLA · USC

Visit Universal Studios, a theme park of dreams and fantasy; Hollywood, the city of movies; a campus tour of top US university UCLA; and USC, a cradle of global talent — plus Griffith Observatory and Santa Monica.

Austin

UT Austin · Samsung Taylor Plant · State Capitol

Visit UT Austin, one of the three "Ivy Leagues" of Texas, and the Texas State Capitol in Austin, the state capital and seat of government.

Houston

NASA Space Center · Rice University

Tour Space Center Houston, NASA's world-class space science museum, and Rice University, the "Harvard of the South."

San Antonio

River Walk · Mission San José · The Alamo

A city where you meet the history of Texas. Depending on the season, we visit SeaWorld or Six Flags.

College Station

Texas A&M · George Bush Museum

Visit Texas A&M University, No. 1 in the world in petroleum engineering, and the George Bush Museum, dedicated to the 41st President of the United States. This is the camp's home base.

LIVING

Housing · Transportation · Meals

Housing
Students stay in an American-style apartment, 2 per room (apartment-style dormitory). Boys and girls are housed separately, and international-student mentors look after each student's daily life.
Amenities include an on-site swimming pool, gym, outdoor grill area, basketball court and volleyball court
Transportation
The travel team's dedicated vehicles are the main means of transportation, and school rides are provided to and from school.
Public transportation may be used in some cities; downtown, we get around on foot or by bus
Meals
Breakfast is a local-style breakfast (bread · milk · oatmeal · soup, etc.), and lunch and dinner are Korean food (special meals are local food). The center provides packed lunches, which students eat at school.
Meal duty (dishes · table setting) is assigned, and students prepare and clean up meals together under mentor guidance · Local meals out are offered as a cultural experience (not included in the fee), and students go shopping for personal snacks once every 2 weeks
