ABOUT

All-Day Intensive English Training

INTO ABROAD's Philippines English Camp offers high-quality English classes with native-speaker teachers. It is an integrated camp of study, daily life and cultural experiences, held in quiet, safe Dasmariñas City, about 30 km from Manila.

KEY POINT

Six Highlights of This Camp

6 Hours a Day with Native-Speaker Teachers

Level-based ESL and Academic English courses systematically improve English skills and study ability.

24-Hour EOP

An English Only Policy environment, using only English 24 hours a day, for natural immersion.

Close Academic Monitoring

Teachers provide individual mentoring based on level tests, regular assessments, and attendance and study-attitude tracking.

Character & Life Growth Program

Life education rooted in community living and a variety of activities build self-management skills and good character.

Safe Life Abroad

Security systems · CCTV · healthy meals · daily-life support from staff provide a safe, comfortable environment.

Cultural Activities & Sightseeing

A Manila tour, a variety of cultural experiences, an amusement park and swimming pool outings combine learning with fun.

Sightseeing in the Philippines

Manila City

The capital of the Philippines and its political, economic and cultural center, where colonial history and a modern city exist side by side.

Tagaytay

A resort town famous for its cool climate and volcanic scenery, with views overlooking Taal Volcano.

Villa Escudero

A place to experience traditional culture in nature, including the unique experience of dining beneath a waterfall.

Violet's Pool

A nature-friendly private swimming spot — a relaxing place to rest and unwind.
