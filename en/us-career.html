About the Program

This is the Young Adventure Program (YAP) held at Texas A&M University. Designed for talented students, it is a specialized summer camp created to encourage career exploration in a wide range of fields, from architecture to veterinary medicine.
Classes are led by Texas A&M faculty and industry experts, and combine introductions to key concepts · group discussions · hands-on learning.

Session 1 (Jul 12 – Jul 17) — Engineering · Medicine · Arts

  • Engineering Design – Spark! — Learn modern engineering technologies such as 3D printing and robotics, and develop engineering thinking by designing, building and testing your own ideas
  • Entomology — A life-scientist experience: build your own insect collection through outdoor collecting and lab analysis, and explore applications all the way to forensics and medicine
  • VR & Game Design (★New) — A metaverse creator course: code your own 3D virtual worlds and mini games using Meta Quest and professional engines
  • Performance Theater — A complete performing-arts course: practice acting, stage design and the whole production process, and put on your own stage showcase
  • Veterinary Medicine — An authentic veterinarian training program, from preparing for vet school admission to observing large animals and practicing small-animal surgery (age 13+)
  • Aerospace Engineering (Camp SOAR) — Design and build with researchers from A&M's Department of Aerospace Engineering, and tour cutting-edge research facilities such as a hypersonic wind tunnel (for incoming Korean grade 11 – grade 12, high school 2 – 3)

Session 2 (Jul 19 – Jul 24) — Advanced Tech · Psychology · Chemistry

  • Biotechnology — Hands-on practice in genetic profiling and molecular imaging with state-of-the-art equipment (age 15+)
  • Mechanical Engineering Design (★New) — Experience the essence of mechanical engineering by designing, building and operating your ideas, from simple machines to complex devices
  • Investigative Forensics — A CSI experience: solve a mock crime scene with scientific investigation techniques such as fingerprinting and bloodstain analysis
  • Psychological Sciences (★New) — An authentic introduction to psychology: explore brain science, social psychology and even how modern technology affects the mind, and learn the principles of human thought and behavior
  • Smash Chemistry — A course for future green chemists: learn "mechanochemistry" and observe molecular changes with an atomic microscope (age 15+)
  • Digital Painting (★New) — An artist course: render light and color digitally with Photoshop and Procreate and complete your own artwork
