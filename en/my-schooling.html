Global education and growth
at an international school over the break

The Schooling Camp (students attend real classes at a local school) is a program held every January, during the winter break for Korean elementary, middle and high schools, in which students briefly join classes alongside enrolled students at Straits International School Rawang, Malaysia, while its term is in session. Without going through formal admission, students can experience international education and a global network under the school's excellent teaching staff and class system.
